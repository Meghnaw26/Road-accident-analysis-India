# Road-accident-analysis-India

## Project Type: Data Analyst (EDA and Visualization)

## About Dataset: The data contains Road accident information for **50 cities** of India in the year **2020**

## Project Overview:
Exploratory data analysis of road accident reports focused on identifying patterns by location, cause, and outcome. The goal is to analyze the causes of road accidents in million-plus-cities in india, identify patterns in causes and outcomes and visualize the distribution of accidents based on different categories.

## Tools and Technologies 
- Python (pandas, numpy)
- Jupyter Notebook
- Visualization: matplotlib, seaborn
- Dashboarding: Tableau (storyboard / dashboard)

## Data Preprocessing 
- Performed an initial data audit to identify missing values, duplicates and inconsistent entries.
- Handled missing values: imputed numeric fields with median values.

## Key Insights
- Chennai leads in total accidents with a staggering 78,459 reported incidents, while Delhi tops the chart in fatal accidents with 72,526 deaths.
- The cause category distribution suggests that weather conditions, traffic violations, junction issues, and impacting vehicles/objects are the major causes of accidents, each contributing 17.5% of the total.
- The majority of road incidents result in minor injuries (18.18%), followed by total injuries (25.53%), with fatalities accounting for 8.21%.
- Accidents with weather-related causes and road features (e.g., straight roads and junctions) tend to have higher injury rates.
- The Top 10 accident subcategories indicate that conditions like sunny/clear weather, over-speeding, and uncontrolled vehicles are dominant factors in accidents.
- Cities such as Chennai and Delhi report higher numbers of fatalities, underlining the need for enhanced safety measures in these regions.
- A deeper dive into the Tree Map for cause hierarchy shows that issues like junction traffic, road features (straight roads, curves), and poor weather conditions are critical areas requiring attention.
- Most of the incidents are non-fatal but result in significant injuries, with a large number leading to severe injuries or persons killed.


## Recommendations
1.	Improvement of Road Infrastructure:
- Better Traffic Control: Strengthen traffic control mechanisms in accident-prone cities, focusing on high-traffic junctions and problematic road features.
- Better Signage and Markings: Improve road visibility, especially during adverse weather conditions like fog and rain, to reduce accidents caused by poor weather.


2.	Enhanced Traffic Law Enforcement:
- Address the high number of traffic violations by increasing police presence in accident hotspots, implementing more speed cameras, and promoting stricter penalties for traffic offenses.


3.	Public Awareness Campaigns:
- Launch educational campaigns to raise awareness about the dangers of speeding, ignoring traffic signals, and the importance of vehicle maintenance. Targeted outreach can particularly focus on regions with high accident rates like Chennai and Delhi.


4.	Technological Interventions:
- Smart Traffic Management Systems: Implement smart traffic management systems at high-accident junctions and routes to regulate flow, prevent congestion, and reduce accidents.
- Real-time Weather Alerts: Integrate weather-related accident prevention measures, such as real-time weather alerts or automatic road condition updates, to help drivers make safer decisions.


5.	Fatality Reduction Initiatives:
- Focus on enhancing vehicle safety standards and improving emergency response to reduce the number of fatalities during accidents. Encouraging the use of seatbelts and helmets should be prioritized.


6.	City-Specific Solutions:
- Chennai: Given its high accident rate, it's critical to focus on junction redesigns and improving road features to mitigate collisions.
- Delhi: A comprehensive approach is needed to address fatal accidents, with an emphasis on both law enforcement and public education.


7.	Invest in Road Maintenance:
- Regular Road Inspections: Ensure the roads are well-maintained, with regular checks for potholes, debris, and other hazardous conditions, which can contribute to accidents, especially in rain or poor weather conditions.


8.	Data-Driven Policy Making:
- Continuously monitor and analyze road accident data from different cities to refine safety policies, ensuring that interventions are targeted where they are most needed based on real-time data trends.
